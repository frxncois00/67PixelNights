ACTION VISUAL FIXES — 2026-11-06
Saved in Assets/Scenes/SampleScene.unity.

Implemented
- All axe/pickaxe/sheep-harvest actions use the upright neutral worker and a separate target-directed tool.
- Mining aims at the selected stone's HitPoint, not merely the last movement vector.
- The swing settles on the target heading. Axe and pickaxe use their correct, different artwork orientations.
- Tools are enlarged for readability. Horizontal attack sheets no longer replace the character during these actions.
- NearbyStoneIndicator labels the nearest solid Rock_* within 1.5 units of its collider surface.
  Yellow = mineable, with [R] and remaining health when facing it.
  Other prompts distinguish needing the axe, facing the rock, and SOLID ROCK scenery.
  The indicator is on Foreground so the player's body does not hide it.
- Eating raises the food sprite toward the mouth, pulses/tilts it and shrinks it over 0.9 seconds.
  F and inventory USE FOOD share the existing successful transaction. Inventory closes after successful use to expose the animation.
  Busy prevents duplicate actions; interruption/death cleans up the transient food object.
- Cooking displays a bobbing/turning meat sprite above the existing campfire, browns it with progress and draws rising steam.
  Fire-out pauses animation and hides steam. Cancellation/completion hides the visual.
  Recipe timing and ingredient/output transactions remain controlled solely by CampfireCooking.
- Inventory runtime slots are now serialized so a script/domain reload during Play Mode preserves them.
  This fixes the observed historical null-slot exceptions affecting tool selection and HUD resource counts.
