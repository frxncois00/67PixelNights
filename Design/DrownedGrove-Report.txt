1. BIOME CONCEPT & LAYOUT LOOP
------------------------------
The Drowned Grove has been upgraded into "The Sunken Waterworks" — an old lake
waterworks settlement where the upper headwater pumps failed, the lower sluice
workshops flooded, and amphibious lake creatures moved into the reeds, shallow
waterline, and stone machinery.

Preserved Existing Architecture:
- Kept the existing Timber Cabin (-25.50, 20.75), western/eastern terrace stairs,
  southern plank bridge, western plank bridge, northern plank crossing, cliffs,
  waterfalls, and surrounding forest rim.

Full Loop Route:
  South Road Entrance (-26.5, 4.5)
    -> 1. Fisherman's Landing (-26.5, 8.5)
    -> 2. The Sluice Shaft & Inspection Walkway (-27.25, 16.15)
    -> 3. Pumpkeeper's Cabin & Bounded Sluice Channel (-23.8, 21.0 / -18.0, 16.0)
    -> 4. Flooded Pump Station Boss Arena (-12.0, 10.9)
    -> 5. The Gauge Shrine Central Islet Stepping Stones (-17.0, 13.0)
          OR Southern Plank Bridge (-18.5, 6.5)
    -> Back to Fisherman's Landing (-26.5, 8.5)

2. FIVE UPGRADED LANDMARKS & INTERACTIVE MECHANICS
--------------------------------------------------
A. South Entrance & West Bank — Fisherman's Landing (EnvironmentExpansion/DrownedGrove/FishermansLanding)
   - Coherent shoreline work area featuring a 5-plank timber pier extending over
     the water (-25.5..-23.4, 9.0) with 4 rope-tied Mooring Posts, a Broken Boat
     Ramp & half-sunken skiff (-24.25, 7.75), 2 A-frame Fishing/Net Drying Racks
     (-28.35, 8.85 and -28.35, 7.25), Tackle Crate, Salted Bait Barrel, Salvage
     pickup (-27.7, 8.1), and a warm Entrance Dock Lantern (-25.5, 5.0).
   - Preserves a wide unobstructed dry combat corridor (x = -27.2..-25.0) for
     fighting and retreating from Reed Stalker ambushes.

B. West Ruins Below the Cabin — The Sluice Shaft (EnvironmentExpansion/DrownedGrove/TheSluiceShaft)
   - Converted the dark stone arch at (-27.25, 17.0) into The Sluice Shaft with
     an iron Sluice Shaft Grate, damp water stains, and an old carved Stone
     Depth Marker (-25.45, 16.85).
   - Attached SluiceShaftEntrance (InteractionTarget): pressing [E] unlatches
     and lifts the iron shaft grate and connects directly to the enclosed lower
     stone maintenance chamber & raised Stone/Grate Inspection Walkway
     (x = -28.8..-25.2, y = 14.8..16.6).
   - Houses the interactive Sluice Drainage Wheel (SluiceDrainageWheel at
     -25.65, 16.15), the shaft supply cache (Chest_SluiceShaft at -28.35, 16.05),
     and 2 mineable slate nodes (-28.6, 13.8 and -26.0, 14.2), guarded by
     Sluice Crabs.

C. Upper Terrace & Waterfall — Pumpkeeper's Cabin (EnvironmentExpansion/DrownedGrove/PumpkeepersCabin)
   - Preserved the cabin (-25.5, 20.75) and stairs (-22.5, 17.5).
   - Dressed the yard with the Main Pump Housing (-23.8, 22.15), 4 Headwater
     Flume Pipes (-22.4..-19.5, 22.15) feeding toward the upper waterfall,
     Pumpkeeper's Workbench (-24.1, 19.95), Water-Level Gauge Markings, and
     Chest_PumpkeepersCabin (-21.0, 22.8).
   - Readable Environmental Clue: WaterworksDiagramClue (InteractionTarget) on
     the Waterworks Diagram Board (-23.15, 20.05) beside the workbench hints at
     turning the Sluice Shaft wheel below the stairs to open the spillway grate
     and reach the shaft cache.
   - Bounded Sluice Spillway Channel (-18.0, 16.0): Located right at the foot of
     the cabin stairs and waterfall (readable directly from the Sluice Shaft
     walkway). Turning the Drainage Wheel ([E]) visibly rotates the wheel, lifts
     the iron Spillway Sluice Grate, drains the high turbid water to expose a
     stone-lined channel bed, switches the water-level markers from flood-amber
     to drained-cyan, and updates the channel's Water tilemap cells to
     WaterUnderBridge (ColliderType.None) + ForestNavigation.Invalidate() so
     players can walk straight across the drained channel.

D. East Bank — Flooded Pump Station (EnvironmentExpansion/DrownedGrove/FloodedPumpStation)
   - Replaced the old stone ring with a recognizable Flooded Pump Station ruin:
     a half-submerged Broken Waterwheel at the lake edge (-14.15, 11.35) with
     animated water ripples, a monumental stone-and-iron Pump Station Sluice
     Gate bulkhead (-12.0, 12.55) with hoist chains and stone piers, and a
     3x3 Raised Stone & Timber Service Platform (-13.0..-11.0, 9.6..11.5).
   - Cleared the two pinching East Bank boulders to provide a clean dodge arena
     with routes back to both bridges and the Gauge Shrine stepping stones.
   - Houses The Floodgate Keeper boss encounter (-12.0, 10.9),
     Chest_FloodedPumpStation (-10.45, 11.75), and PumpStation_Salvage (-10.4, 9.1).

E. Central Islet — The Gauge Shrine (EnvironmentExpansion/DrownedGrove/TheGaugeShrine)
   - Turned the central 2x2 lake islet (-18..-16, 12..14) into The Gauge Shrine
     with a carved Stone Water Gauge & Brass Lantern Monument (-17.35, 13.45),
     warm shrine lantern (-17.65, 12.35), and reward stash Chest_GaugeShrine
     (-16.35, 13.45).
   - Connected the islet with walkable mossy limestone Stepping Stones eastward
     to the Flooded Pump Station (-15.65..-14.65, 12.15..12.95) and southward
     to the southern peninsula/bridge (-16.55..-16.35, 10.75..11.55) using
     WaterUnderBridge (ColliderType.None) so both player movement and
     ForestNavigation enemy pathfinding cross reliably while preserving open
     lake water around the islet.

3. LAKE CREATURE ROSTER (13 PROXIMITY SLOTS, LOCAL CAP 5, GLOBAL CAP 10)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Replaced all rogue-style encounters in The Drowned Grove with 4 custom lake
creatures (Assets/Scripts/Environment/SunkenWaterworksCreature.cs):

1) Reed Stalker (Assets/DrownedGrove/Enemies/reed_stalker.asset, 5 slots)
   - Placement: Fisherman's Landing & bankside reed beds.
   - Behavior: Hides crouched in bankside reeds (68% alpha camouflage + reed
     cluster overlay). Shaking reeds and an expanding water ripple ring warn the
     player (0.60s windup) before a short lunge (1.55u) onto dry ground.
   - Night Tell: Chartreuse bioluminescent predator eyes + subtle Point Light 2D.

2) Sluice Crab (Assets/DrownedGrove/Enemies/sluice_crab.asset, 5 slots)
   - Placement: Sluice Shaft walkway, cabin terrace, and Pump Station approaches.
   - Behavior: Guards its front with its copper-and-slate shell (frontal hits
     take 0.35x damage with a cyan shell-deflect flash). Executes a slow claw
     strike (0.68s windup) and then exposes its vulnerable side for 1.65s
     (taking 1.35x bonus damage with an amber weak-side indicator).
   - Night Tell: Bioluminescent cyan-amber carapace vents + Point Light 2D.

3) Lantern Eel (Assets/DrownedGrove/Enemies/lantern_eel.asset, 2 slots)
   - Placement: Shallow waterline beside The Gauge Shrine islet (-16.1, 12.3)
     and Flooded Pump Station (-13.6, 10.6).
   - Behavior: Tethered to the shallow shoreline edge; projects a clearly marked
     rectangular water-surge lane (length 2.75u, width 0.75u) across a narrow
     strip of shoreline during its 0.85s windup, then surfaces at the bank edge
     for 2.20s so melee and bow attacks reliably hit its collider (1.35x damage
     while surfaced).
   - Night Tell: Bright golden-cyan angler lantern lure orb above the water.

4) The Floodgate Keeper — Boss (Assets/DrownedGrove/Enemies/floodgate_keeper.asset, 1 slot)
   - Placement: Flooded Pump Station arena (-12.0, 10.9), 135 HP, 16 damage.
   - Behavior: Alternates a close-range paddle-cleaver sweep with a telegraphed
     water surge (length 3.6u, width 0.95u) down one lane of the pump platform.
     Exposes its pressurized Hydro-Core weak point for 2.20s after each surge
     (taking 1.50x weak-point damage). Drops FloodgateKeeperSpoils (Scrap, Stone,
     Wood, Berries) upon defeat via AshenFrontierEncounterController.
   - Night Tell: Pressurized cyan-gold Hydro-Core & helm visor beacon.
