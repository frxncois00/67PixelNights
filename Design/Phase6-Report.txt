1. COMPLETED SURVIVAL SYSTEMS
- Health: 100 maximum; controlled damage/healing; one death event; HUD and warnings.
- Hunger: 100 maximum; drains 0.2/second; zero hunger causes 5 damage every 5 seconds.
- Berry: restores 15 hunger. Cooked meat: restores 35 hunger and 5 health.
  Raw meat is not directly edible. Food stacks to 20 in the existing inventory.
- Four existing bushes provide E berry gathering with overflow protection and
  a 90-second respawn timer.
- Three passive sheep wander on valid ground. Two successful harvest hits produce
  a single pickup stack of 1-2 raw meat. No automatic sheep respawn.
- C cooks one raw meat into one cooked meat in five seconds near a burning fire.
  Leaving range cancels; extinguishing pauses; relighting resumes. Ingredients
  are spent only in the successful atomic completion transaction.
- Death locks player actions/inventory, cancels cooking and offers scene restart.
  Restart resets session state. No disk persistence or enemy combat was added.

2. RECENT VISUAL AND RELIABILITY CHANGES
- Axe, pickaxe and sheep-harvest animations retain the upright neutral worker.
  A separate equipped-tool sprite aims toward the selected target; mining uses
  the stone's HitPoint instead of merely the player's previous movement vector.
- F and inventory USE FOOD now play a 0.9-second eating animation: the food moves
  near the mouth, tilts/pulses and shrinks. Successful inventory use closes the
  inventory so the animation is visible. Busy/death/interruption guards remain.
- Cooking shows turning/bobbing meat, browning and rising steam above the fire.
  Extinguishing pauses the visual and steam; cancellation/completion cleans up.
- NearbyStoneIndicator references 14 existing Rock_* objects, measures proximity
  to collider surfaces and renders above the player. It distinguishes mineable
  rocks, remaining hits, wrong equipment, facing requirements and SOLID ROCK.
  Only configured resource nodes are mineable; decorative rocks remain solid.
- Runtime inventory slots are serialized to survive script/domain reloads in
  Play Mode, fixing observed null-slot errors. This is NOT a save system.

3. EQUIPMENT AND HAND PLACEMENT — CURRENT BEHAVIOR
- Tools must be owned, moved into a hotbar slot and selected before use.
- Basic Axe (basic_axe): Space/left-click chops trees; E harvests sheep.
- Basic Pickaxe (basic_pickaxe): R mines configured stone nodes.
- Short Sword (basic_sword): E harvests sheep; cannot chop trees or mine stone.
  Uses Tiny Swords Tool_02, an existing short-blade sprite, not a long sword.
- Wrong-tool and missing-target attempts now show an explanation instead of
  silently doing nothing. The latest reported chopping failure was inspected:
  basic_sword was equipped, and input was not blocked. Axe chopping passed tests.
- Neutral Pawn idle/walk clips replace frames with a permanently drawn axe.
  Player/Equipped Tool displays the actual selected tool and hides during actions.
- Tool scale is 1, reduced from 1.8. ToolGripPose supplies per-tool grip positions
  and head angles. Idle tools mirror with the player; swings rotate around the
  same hand anchor instead of floating near the feet or orbiting away.
- Equipment changes are blocked during a swing. Tools can be dropped/recollected.

E. HEALTH AND HUNGER
Health starts at 100, maximum 100.
Public read-only properties: CurrentHealth, MaxHealth, NormalizedHealth, IsAlive, IsDead, CauseOfDeath.
TakeDamage(amount, cause), Heal(amount), and development-only SetHealthForTesting provide controlled mutation.
Changed, Damaged, Healed and Died events are exposed. Death fires once.
Negative/nonfinite damage and healing are rejected. Dead players cannot heal back to life; use restart.

Hunger starts at 100, maximum 100, depletion 0.2/second.
At zero, starvation deals 5 health per 5 seconds.
Advance(seconds) separates time spent depleting positive hunger from time actually spent starving.
Large deltas apply the exact accumulated interval damage in one bounded health call.
Restore(amount) clamps hunger and resets starvation timing when hunger becomes positive.
Hunger uses scaled Time.deltaTime, continues with inventory open and stops damaging after death.
Low-hunger (20%), starving (0) and low-health (25%) warnings fire on threshold entry, not every frame.
Development SetHungerForTesting is available for manual/automated tests.

H. COOKING AND TRANSACTION SAFETY
Existing Campfire has CampfireCooking.
C starts cooking within 2 units, while alive, with a burning fire and one raw meat.
Cooking duration 5 seconds; one job at a time.
No ingredient is consumed while the timer runs.
InventoryController.CanExchangeOne checks whether output can fit, including a slot freed by consuming a singleton ingredient.
ExchangeOne removes one raw meat and inserts one cooked meat before emitting a single inventory change event.
Leaving range cancels without spending.
Extinguished fire pauses elapsed cooking; relighting resumes while the player stays in range.
Death or component disable cancels.
If ingredients disappear or output capacity is lost before completion, the transaction fails without consuming anything.
Successful completion produces exactly one cooked meat.
The cooking HUD is below the campfire HUD; refuel E and upgrade U remain unchanged.
