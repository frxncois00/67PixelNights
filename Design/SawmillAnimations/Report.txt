SAWMILL WEAPON ANIMATION SHEETS — 2026-11-09

Completed and integrated in Assets/Scenes/SampleScene.unity.

Weapons: Wood Knife, Wood Spear, Wood Shovel, Wood Long Sword,
Wood Long Hammer, Wood Bow. Wooden Fence is excluded. Basic axe,
basic pickaxe and basic short sword continue to use their existing animations.

Assets created
- Assets/PixelCrawler/BodyA/SawmillWeapons/<weapon>/:
  54 layered .aseprite sources and 54 transparent PNG sheets, with Unity .meta files.
  Each weapon has Idle, Walk and Attack in Down, Up and Side views.
  Side is mirrored for left; front and back have their own poses.
  Idle: 4 frames. Walk: 6 frames. Attack: 8 frames. Total: 324 sliced sprites.
  96x96 cells, 32 PPU, Point, uncompressed PNGs, pivot (0.5, 1/3).
- Assets/Animations/Player/SawmillWeapons/<weapon>/: 54 .anim clips and metadata.
- Assets/Scripts/Player/SawmillWeaponAnimations.cs and metadata.
- Asset folder README.md and manifest.json describe source/export conventions.
- AgentScripts/aseprite_layers.py: RGBA source reader/writer.
- AgentScripts/build_sawmill_sheets.py: reproducible layered authoring/export.
- AgentScripts/SetupSawmillAnimations.cs: Unity slicing, clips and scene integration.
- AgentScripts/TestSawmillAnimations.cs and TestSawmillWalking.cs: Editor validation.

Existing files modified
- Assets/Scripts/Inventory/EquippedToolVisual.cs: use authored held/walk frames
  for these six items and hide the separate floating item renderer.
- Assets/Scripts/Gathering/PlayerGatherAnimation.cs: play the authored attack
  frames for these six items through the existing action and restoration logic.
- Assets/Scenes/SampleScene.unity: one SawmillWeaponAnimations component added
  to Player, with all six frame sets assigned. Scene saved in Edit Mode.

Art and behavior
- Reused Body_A legs, torso, head and native arm poses from the installed pack.
- Replaced weapon layers with existing sawmill icon artwork; aligned the grip
  to the leading hand per frame. Hand layers occlude the grip correctly.
- Walking uses the original animated legs with the equipped arm/weapon pose
  following the torso. Bow arms/draw/release use matching skin-palette pixels.
- Front, side and rear poses include appropriate body/weapon overlap.
- Weapon dimensions are consistent between held, walking and attack sheets.
- Hit timing and total attack durations retain the existing weapon profiles.
- Existing damage, cooldowns, ownership, equip controls and arrow spawning remain
  authoritative. The old procedural weapon overlay is bypassed for these six
  authored sets; attacks now animate the character's body, arms and weapon together.
- Runtime uses the component's sprite arrays. The .anim clips are also supplied
  for preview/editing, rather than replacing the existing Player Animator controller.

Use
Press Play, craft/pick up a sawmill weapon, equip it with its hotbar key, and move
with WASD/arrows. Space/click plays its attack animation. No manual Inspector
setup is required. Edit layered sources under the asset folder; follow README
before regenerating so manual pixel changes are not overwritten.
