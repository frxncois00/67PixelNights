- Population Caps & Spawn Controls (AshenFrontierEncounterController):
  - Proximity Activation: Enemies do NOT all spawn simultaneously; each slot
    activates only when the player enters its activationRadius (9.5u - 11.5u).
  - Local Concurrent Cap: maxConcurrentLocalEnemies = 6.
  - Global Cap Respect: Checks existingSpawner.ActiveCount + LocalActiveCount
    against existingSpawner.maxActiveEnemies (10), never exceeding the global
    cap.
  - Safe Spawn Validation: Verifies existingSpawner.Walkable, CampfireSafeZone,
    water avoidance, and minimum player distance before spawning.
  - Leash & Despawn: Unengaged enemies beyond despawnDistance (26u) despawn
    cleanly back to their slot so closer encounters can activate; defeated slots
    stay cleared for the day and reset on DayNightCycle.OnDayStarted.
  - Night Waves Intact: Existing night-wave schedule and EnemySpawner logic are
    untouched; 3 walkable open-ground night markers are registered via
    existingSpawner.RegisterSpawnPoint.

9. SUBSTITUTIONS, LIMITATIONS & REMAINING WORK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Ranged Enemy AI Substitution: The project has no enemy ranged projectile AI
  (EnemyController and EnemyArchetype only implement melee chase/lunge/slam
  profiles). Per instructions ("Ranged guards only if working ranged enemy AI
  already exists... No inaccessible enemy placed on a purely decorative tower"),
  the Ruined Watchpost is guarded by accessible ground-level melee Rogues
  (Watchpost_Rogue_01 and Watchpost_Raider_02), and the Watchtower top is kept
  purely decorative.
- Destructible Barriers: The project does not have a world-destructible
  barricade component for player attacks (PlaceableStructure is for player-built
  camp defenses). Timber barricades are therefore placed as solid partial cover
  with wide openings around every landmark so movement and retreat routes are
  never blocked.
- Quarry Elevation: Since the project uses flat 2D navigation without multi-
  level elevation colliders, Quarry rim rock formations provide visual depth
  around the basin while keeping the entire quarry floor walkable.
