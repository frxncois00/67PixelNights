K. STRUCTURE ATTACKS — PASS CONTROLLED PLAY MODE TESTS
Only PlaceableStructure with a definition and live collider can be attacked. First solid obstruction on the ray to the intended player must be that structure. Original houses, cliffs, decorative objects, campfire and workbench lack this damage target.
Prefer a found detour. Attack allowed when no bounded route succeeds or route length exceeds1.8x direct distance+2 units, and the blocking structure is within attack range. This is the documented bounded detour policy, not optimal path proof.
Hit rechecks target existence/health, first obstruction, closest-point range, line of sight, night/enemy/player alive and enemy outside protection. Navigation revision during a structure windup cancels the stale attack. Damage uses PlaceableStructure.TakeDamage once per hit.
Destroying a structure uses existing collider removal/navigation invalidation. Nonempty chest wrecks retain withdraw-only contents. No duplicate structure health or inventory was added.
Test fixture used a temporary12-unit-wide fence collider to create a reproducible blocked corridor; production footprint remained unchanged. This is not a claim that every hand-built arrangement passed.
Calculated earliest fence defeat (one enemy, uninterrupted): Stalker12.35s, Wolf13.15s, Crawler10.6s, Brute3.8s, Ancient4s. Brute vs150HP barricade12.8s; Ancient10s. These are arithmetic estimates, not timed endurance measurements. Multi-enemy defense balance still needs manual playtesting.
