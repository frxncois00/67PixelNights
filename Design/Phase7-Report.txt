E. AI STATE MACHINE
Idle pauses, Patrol selects nearby goals, Chase follows detected unprotected
players, Search visits last seen location for three seconds, Retreat moves away
from active protection, Attack winds up/revalidates/recovers, Dead stops damage.
Defaults: health 30; patrol 1.2; chase 2; detection 5; attack range 1; damage 10;
cooldown 2; windup .35; recovery .3. Inspector configuration is exposed.
Movement samples five steering directions with ground-footprint, overlap and
linecast checks. This is conservative local steering, not full pathfinding.

F. SPAWNING
Existing OnNightStarted/OnDayStarted events drive spawning and cleanup.
256 bounded attempts per requested enemy. Reject protected areas, occupied
footprints, missing Ground tiles and positions within six units of the player.
Unavailable placements warn and skip. Sunrise deactivates immediately, destroys
remaining enemies and clears the tracked list; no spawn coroutine can overlap.
Spawn accessibility beyond local footprint is NOT guaranteed by connected-region
navigation. Narrow passages/disconnected ground require further acceptance work.

G. DIFFICULTY
Night 1 count 2; +1 each night; maximum 10, bounded using long arithmetic.
Health/damage growth omitted (optional); base stats stay predictable.

H. SAFE ZONE
Uses CampfireSafeZone.IsPositionSafe, IsPlayerSafe and CurrentSafeRadius.
No duplicate radius or lighting-based protection. Attack validation rechecks
protection at hit time. Relit enemies attempt outward movement through valid
terrain; trapped enemies may need better route finding (not teleportation).

I. SWORD COMBAT
Owned/equipped basic_sword only; Space/click routed through original handler.
15 damage, range 1.5, 100-degree facing arc, cooldown .6; busy/input/death gates.
Each tracked enemy evaluated once per swing; linecast rejects blocked hits.
Damage occurs on the existing gathering animation completion callback.
Knockback uses bounded .2-second movement at configurable strength 2.5, with
terrain/obstacle/protection checks. No sheep/resource combat crossover.

J. TOOL VISUALS
ToolGripPose, EquippedToolVisual, scale 1, neutral Pawn locomotion and the
existing short blade are unchanged. Sword animation uses PlayerGatherAnimation.
Empty initial inventory and all three original tool pickups retained.

K. PLAYER HEALTH / DEATH
Damage uses PlayerHealth.TakeDamage with Forest Stalker cause.
PlayerDamageFlash provides a brief red tint and restores the prior color.
Enemy attacks validate alive state, night, range, protection and line of sight
at the actual hit time. Dead players are no longer targeted.
Existing game-over/restart code was not rewritten.
