CHEST OPENING ANIMATION
Completed: 8 November 2026
Scene: Assets/Scenes/SampleScene.unity
Unity: 6000.6.4f1

IMPLEMENTED
- Press E near an unopened loot chest to collect its contents and play a 0.65-second opening animation.
- The lid lifts, five small golden pixel sparkles rise and fade, and the lid stays open afterward.
- Empty chests retain the existing dimmed appearance. Chests with uncollected loot stay usable.
- Loot remains in the chest when inventory space is unavailable. Returning later collects the remainder without rerolling rewards or replaying the first-open animation.
- Player movement continues during the animation. No new input binding, gameplay pause, or animation package was added.
- Disabling an animation/chest stops the transient effect and preserves its open state.
- The effect only runs while opening; it has no idle Update loop and creates no objects per frame.

CONFIGURED
All five current LootChest instances and the reusable LootChest prefab:
- Chest_Quarry
- Chest_Camp
- Chest_Ruins
- Dark Forest Supply Chest (Wooden Cache Placeholder)
- Abandoned Cabin Supply Chest (Wooden Cache Placeholder)

The three outpost chests retain the original Cute Fantasy closed chest sprite. Its existing PNG is sliced into body/lid pieces for opening, with an interior and small sparkles. No replacement/composite PNG was generated. The two older supply caches animate their existing separate lid artwork.
SortingGroup keeps each chest's body, lid and sparkles together while using the existing Objects sorting layer. Object locations, loot quantities and interaction range were not changed by this feature.
