PHASE 9 — ALREADY IMPLEMENTED
Crafting at a workbench near (-1.5,2), contextual E, default range 2.
Recipes (one output each):
Wooden Fence = 3 wood; 50 HP; stack 20.
Reinforced Barricade = 5 wood + 2 stone; 150 HP; stack 20.
Storage Chest = 6 wood + 2 scrap; 50 HP; stack 10; 12 storage slots.
Repair Kit = 2 wood + 1 scrap; repairs up to 25 HP; stack 20.
Atomic multi-ingredient crafting checks final output capacity before committing one inventory change. No spending on failed recipes.
Equip building item, close inventory, select hotbar slot, B preview, left-click place, right-click/Escape cancel. Range 3; half-unit snapping; green/red ghost and footprint outline; no ghost collider.
Placement checks terrain, solid overlaps, player, main routes, interaction proximity and simulated route connectivity. Commit revalidates and consumes one item.
E on a structure opens management. Repair spends one kit only if useful. Dismantle requires confirmation, no refund; nonempty chests reject dismantling.
Chest deposit/withdraw one or stack supports partial capacity. Destroyed nonempty chest remains a nonblocking withdraw-only wreck, retaining contents.
New building/world artwork and icons are placeholders: a wood-log image can mean a chest or fence, while stone represents the barricade. Use the item name to distinguish them. Ordinary wood cannot itself be placed as a chest/fence.
Runtime structures/storage are session-only. No persistence/save system.

CONTROLS
WASD / arrows: movement.
1-5: selected hotbar item.
Space / left click: equipped axe chopping or sword combat.
R: mining with equipped pickaxe.
E: contextual pickup, berry/sheep/campfire interaction, workbench or structure management.
F: eat selected hotbar food; inventory Use Food also supported.
C: cook near burning campfire.
U: campfire upgrade.
Tab / I: inventory; G: drop one.
B: building preview with an equipped building item.
Left click in building mode: placement, not a weapon swing.
Right click / Escape: cancel building; Escape closes modal UI.
F6/F7: development cycle skip/pause.
