Phase 4 | Woodcutting and gathering

The blue worker starts with an axe and an empty shared resource wallet. Face the trunk or rock and tap Space or left-click. An accepted swing plays the existing axe animation and deals one hit on completion. The selection cone follows PlayerMovement.Facing, with side-facing artwork for vertical swings. Trees and rocks flash on hit. On depletion their renderer and solid collider are disabled. Loose drops can be picked up with E. The same E action gathers scrap or feeds the campfire when its prompt is selected.

Scene configuration
Selected existing Tree_08, Tree_12, Tree_22, Tree_25, Tree_31, Tree_34; Rock_03, Rock_07, Rock_13. All other existing trees and rocks remain permanent. Two test scrap piles near (-3,1) and (3,-4) use the pack's Tool_04 sprite as a placeholder. Five pre-existing loose wood objects are pickups. The old F gathering and WoodSource components were removed from the scene. Campfire stone ring and log seats are decorative.
Three health per tree/rock; one damage per axe swing; range 1.5, cooldown .5 s plus swing duration. Wood yield 2–4, stone 1–3, scrap 1–2. Tree/stone/scrap regrowth after 120/180/240 seconds, postponed when occupied or a previous drop remains uncollected. Axe compatibility and yield/timing are Inspector settings. Picks use 1.5-unit range; campfire interaction uses its existing 2-unit range.
E selects nearest available target and breaks equal-distance ties by ordinal object name; all configured targets and generated pickups have distinct names. Resource balances update through wallet events. Existing campfire costs and safety values apply. Resource storage can be adapted to inventory slots later via the typed wallet API.
Optional audio and extra particles omitted. If a drop cannot find a clear position after bounded placement attempts, it logs a warning. Resources are session-local.

Manual acceptance
Open SampleScene, press Play and focus Game. Travel south from camp to Tree_25 near (-4.3,-5.1). Face the trunk and swing three times, tapping once per animation. Step near each log and tap E. Return to the fire and press E when the add wood prompt appears. Press U with five wood. Try the same axe on Rock_03 near (0.6,-4.1); use E directly on a scrap pile. Watch totals and try holding each key to check for repeats. After collecting the drops, wait out regrowth, first standing over the former trunk and then stepping clear. Check camera, collisions, F6/F7 and the night lighting in your usual play session. No manual Inspector wiring required.
