RESOURCE HIT AND HARVEST FEEDBACK
Unity 6000.6.4f1 | SampleScene | 9 November 2026

IMPLEMENTED
- Trees briefly sway, highlight and shed leaf/wood fragments when struck.
- Stones briefly jolt, highlight and shed stone fragments when struck.
- A final hit plays a short fading break/fall reaction.
- Floating +N WOOD / +N STONE labels pop, rise and fade after depletion.
  The number is the actual number of pickups successfully created, not a
  separate random visual number. The smaller DROPPED label distinguishes
  these ground pickups from resources already collected into inventory.
- Existing yield settings, pickup controls, regeneration and weapon attacks
  are retained. Use E to collect the resulting nearby drops as before.
- The feedback moves only a temporary visual. The resource root and physical
  collider remain fixed during hits. Depletion disables the original collider
  through the existing resource logic.
- Hit feedback is inactive between hits. Seven fragments are reused per struck
  node; temporary yield labels destroy themselves after 1.25 seconds.
